## Rules, with every constant and its source

As registered (the check that the harness is faithful): `compute()` with the settings `main()` derives: r_assoc and g from
`noise_floor.json T3_adopted['230613']` (method of `noise_floor.json T7`), g_laser from `lmi_assoc.json` pooled cap 0.15 m,
exclusion `registration_ok`, reference-stop h95, same-day false-change rate re-measured at r_assoc. The gates it returns
must equal `ledger.json gates_T1_T7` of record on every verdict and number; any difference is reported and stops the test.
Constants of the design, unchanged: association cap 0.095 m, growth cap 0.08 m, T4 ratio 0.9, T6 median within g and
agreement >= 0.9 on at least 5 plants, T2 check (soil residual <= 2 cm, reverse candidate <= 0.7), T1 0.70 px.

**Item 30 (T5 specificity).** Text: "report the false-removal rate of each sensor with its interval beside the same-day
rate, and make no pass or fail of it while the rate exceeds 0.5." Computation: for each sensor and pair that carries a
specificity record, false-removal rate = removals called / on-row unchanged candidates, with a 95 % Wilson score interval;
the Clopper-Pearson interval beside it. The interval method is not named in the item: Wilson is my judgement. The plants
are not independent draws (shared registration, row structure), so either interval understates the uncertainty; this is
said beside the numbers. The same-day rate is given with its counts (unmatched / instances in the eroded overlaps, at
r_assoc) and the same interval where the record holds counts. Reading of "the rate": the sensor's same-day false-change
rate (the antecedent of the item's first sentence). Clause verdict: "no verdict" when that rate exceeds 0.5 (strictly);
otherwise the clause as written (removals <= rate x count). When the same-day rate cannot be measured (no overlapping pair
under the exclusion) the clause is "not evaluable", counted with its reason. The other reading ("the rate" = the
false-removal rate) is computed beside and is not the column. T5 overall: fail if a judged clause fails; pass if the
removal clause passes and no judged clause fails, with the unjudged clause named. The laser ceiling's line is reported the
same way; it is not part of the T5 verdict as registered and is not made part of it.

**Item 31 (T7 plant clause).** Text: "test the per-plant difference against the cross-sensor floor M2d (UAV1 against the
laser, per-plant |diff| p95 5.7-14.4 cm), and report the bias (4-8 cm low) as the finding." Computation: per stop that has
a laser date, the same statistic as registered (per-plant |h95 splat minus laser| p95 at the laser footprints, raw,
datum-only, `dsm_vs_laser.json` nview6, adopted method) against the M2d per-plant |diff| p95 of the pair
`laser_<laser date>_vs_<the stop's UAV1 reference>` (`cross_sensor_floor.json per_plant.registered.abs_diff_p95_m`; 230602,
230614, 230623 for the three rig days). A stop passes when its p95 is at or below that floor. Aggregation unchanged from
the registered clause: T7 passes only if every evaluated stop passes; the count is reported. The per-stop median difference
is reported as the bias. Stops without a laser date (230628) are counted as not evaluable, as in the registered clause.
Plot 178 has no M2d of its own: its stops are read against plot 198's floor of the same dates, flagged (the ledger's own
convention for that plot's thresholds), and "not evaluable on its own floor" is the cell's verdict. The soil clause is
untouched (after the M2d offset is removed; by construction).

**Item 32 (stop exclusion).** Text: "Fix in the design which statement governs (T2 check, or T2 check and plant-pattern
check)". Column = the second statement (`--exclude plant_pattern`). Two computations, both written: (b, the column) the
whole chain as it would run if that statement governed: T3_adopted and T7 method from `noise_floor.json
exclusion_variants['plant_pattern']` (the primary set's method where T7 is not decidable, as that record states), then
r_assoc and g by `main()`'s own rule (the day's values when decidable, else the laser's pooled same-day p95 capped at
0.095 m and the laser's |dh95| p95 capped at 0.08 m; for plot 178, plot 198's values under the same statement when
decidable, else plot 178's laser fallback), false-change rate re-measured on that stop set; (a, beside) the stop set alone
with r_assoc and g held at the registered values, which is the line `ledger.json sensitivity.exclusion.plant_pattern`
already holds and must be reproduced. No plant-pattern threshold is changed or proposed.

**Item 33 (T3 wording).** Text: "Read the association verdict from the 0.15 m radius (below the in-row spacing, the laser's
adopted cap) and state the 0.20 m value beside it". Computation: from `T3_adopted['230613'].position_p95_by_radius_m` (the
variant's under all four (b)): "resolvable" if the p95 at 0.15 m is at or below 0.095 m, "not resolvable" if above,
"undecidable" if the day is not decidable (fewer than 5 matched plants at 0.10 m) or has no 0.15 m measurement; the 0.20 m
value and the matched counts beside. The laser's own line (pooled p95 at the 0.15 m cap against 0.095 m) beside. Item 33
does not change r_assoc or g; the ledger line at r_assoc 0.095 m that already exists (`ledger.json sensitivity.r_assoc`)
is quoted beside as a consequence someone might draw, labelled as outside the item.

**All four.** The three clause changes applied to the run under each resolution of item 32.

## What is reported, and the reading

The table (Markdown and JSON); per item: the change in one sentence, the verdicts it moves and by how much, and a
classification made by these criteria, written here before any number: *mis-specified* if the clause compares a quantity
with a threshold that measures a different quantity, judged from the text alone; *trivially satisfiable* if the bound
admits more than half of the candidates being called removed (the item's own 0.5); *internally inconsistent* if two texts
of the record prescribe different things; *merely makes a failure pass* if none of the three holds and a fail turns into a
pass. These classifications are judgements and are labelled so; the sceptical reviewer's reading is written for each item
whatever the table shows. The verdict of this test is the table itself: an item "moves" a verdict if the cell's verdict
word differs from the as-registered cell, and "moves a number only" otherwise.

Marks: the cells that read `marks_<date>.csv` or `premarks_<date>.csv` are identified from the code path (which functions
read marks) and confirmed by a run in which the marks are withheld from `compute()` (an in-memory override; cells that
keep their value are final with respect to the marks). Nothing is substituted for the marks.
